CISSP vs CEH India 2026 — Which Cybersecurity Certification Should You Choose?

August 27, 2026 · 6 min read · cybercourse.in

CISSP vs CEH — Two Different Tracks

CISSP (Certified Information Systems Security Professional) from ISC2 and CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) from EC-Council are the two most recognised cybersecurity certifications in India. They are fundamentally different in career track, difficulty, and target audience — choosing the wrong one wastes 6-18 months of study time.

Choose CISSP When:

  • You target CISO, VP Security, or security management roles
  • You have 5+ years of information security experience across multiple domains
  • Your organisation's security needs are governance, risk, compliance (GRC) focused
  • You are moving from technical security to management track
  • Large enterprise or MNC security roles — CISSP is a near-mandatory filter

Choose CEH When:

  • You want to work as a penetration tester or ethical hacker
  • You are in IT or have basic networking knowledge but under 5 years security exp
  • Bug bounty hunting is a career goal alongside structured employment
  • You prefer a more structured, teachable syllabus (CEH has clear domains)
  • Indian IT services firms (Infosys, TCS, Wipro cybersecurity practices) — CEH is well-recognised

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CISSP or CEH better for India 2026?

Depends on your career track. CISSP is better for management-track security professionals (CISO, VP Security, GRC roles) with 5+ years experience. CEH is better for technical security roles (pen testing, ethical hacking, security analyst) or if you are under 5 years experience. Most senior security leaders eventually hold both.

What is CISSP exam fee in India 2026?

CISSP exam registration fee: approximately $600 (approximately Rs 50,000) paid to ISC2. Add study materials (Official Study Guide: Rs 3,000-5,000) and training course (optional, Rs 20,000-60,000). Total investment approximately Rs 55,000-1,15,000 depending on training choices.

Can I pass CEH without experience?

Technically yes — CEH does not have a strict experience requirement (unlike CISSP which requires 5 years). EC-Council recommends 2 years IT experience. Many candidates pass CEH with less experience using structured study programmes. However, without hands-on experience, translating CEH certification into jobs is more difficult — employers value demonstrated skills alongside the certification.
